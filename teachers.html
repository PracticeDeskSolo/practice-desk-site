For professional musicians & teachers

Teach on your time.

Practice Desk is beginning to build a network of professional musicians who want to share their expertise without the scheduling demands of regular live lessons.

The idea

Thoughtful feedback, without another diary commitment.

Students submit a recording and some context about what they are working on. You review their playing when it suits you and provide considered feedback and practical direction.

There is no requirement to take on regular pupils or reserve a recurring teaching slot. The asynchronous format also lets you replay passages, listen carefully and respond without the technical and sound constraints of a live video call.

How it could work

You stay in control.

Your schedule

Review submissions when it suits you. Choose how many reviews you want to accept and complete them around your own schedule.

Your expertise

Offer feedback in the areas that suit your experience, instrument and teaching interests.

No regular commitment

Take individual reviews without committing to a weekly teaching diary.

Practice Desk handles the platform

Practice Desk provides the customer-facing platform and takes a small commission on completed reviews.

Interested?
